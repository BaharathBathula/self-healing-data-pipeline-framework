# 🧠 Autonomous Reliability Engineering

The framework introduces the concept of Autonomous Reliability Engineering (ARE).

ARE represents a reliability engineering model where systems are capable of:

- monitoring operational behavior
- evaluating reliability risk
- detecting anomalies
- selecting remediation strategies
- validating operational recovery
- adapting reliability policies dynamically

without requiring continuous manual intervention.

This research direction aligns with the broader industry movement toward intelligent autonomous enterprise operations.
