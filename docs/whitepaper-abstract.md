# Whitepaper Abstract

Modern enterprise systems increasingly depend on distributed cloud-native infrastructure, real-time analytics platforms, AI-native operational systems, and large-scale streaming architectures.

As operational complexity continues to grow, traditional reactive monitoring systems become increasingly insufficient for maintaining scalable operational resilience.

This project introduces the Autonomous Self-Healing Data Reliability Control Plane, a reliability engineering framework designed to support intelligent operational resilience through:

- autonomous remediation
- reliability scoring intelligence
- governance-aware operational orchestration
- SLA-driven operational workflows
- self-healing infrastructure systems

The framework introduces a centralized Reliability Control Plane architecture integrating operational telemetry, anomaly classification, remediation orchestration, governance validation, and reliability intelligence into a unified enterprise operational model.

The project explores architectural approaches aligned with next-generation enterprise infrastructure systems including:
- AI-driven operational intelligence
- autonomous infrastructure resilience
- predictive reliability engineering
- intelligent enterprise orchestration

The framework is applicable across industries including:
- Financial Services
- Insurance
- Healthcare
- AI infrastructure environments
- distributed enterprise analytics systems

The project contributes toward the broader evolution of autonomous enterprise reliability engineering and self-healing operational infrastructure architectures.
