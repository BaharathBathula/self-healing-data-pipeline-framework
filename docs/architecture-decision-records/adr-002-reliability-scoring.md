# ADR-002: Reliability Scoring Intelligence Model

## Status

Accepted

---

# Context

Traditional monitoring systems generally expose isolated infrastructure metrics without providing unified operational reliability evaluation.

Enterprise engineering teams often lack:
- centralized operational trust indicators
- reliability prioritization frameworks
- operational risk intelligence
- reliability trend visibility

A measurable reliability evaluation framework was required to support proactive operational resilience.

---

# Decision

The framework adopts a multi-dimensional Reliability Scoring Model evaluating:

- SLA compliance
- data quality
- pipeline stability
- recovery effectiveness
- freshness and latency

Each operational dimension contributes to an aggregated reliability score representing overall pipeline trustworthiness.

---

# Rationale

The scoring model enables:

- operational risk prioritization
- proactive remediation orchestration
- reliability trend analysis
- centralized operational trust evaluation
- intelligent anomaly response workflows

This transforms monitoring from passive telemetry into actionable operational intelligence.

---

# Consequences

## Positive Outcomes

- measurable operational trust
- proactive reliability evaluation
- improved anomaly prioritization
- enhanced operational visibility
- scalable reliability governance

---

## Tradeoffs

- scoring calibration complexity
- dynamic weighting requirements
- operational metric dependency

---

# Significance

The Reliability Scoring Model contributes toward intelligent reliability engineering for modern enterprise infrastructure systems.

The framework supports:
- AI-ready operational resilience
- autonomous remediation orchestration
- scalable operational intelligence
