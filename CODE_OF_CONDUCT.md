# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free experience for everyone participating in this project, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive
- Provide thoughtful technical feedback
- Collaborate professionally
- Focus on improving the project
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or abusive language
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Trolling or disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces including issues, pull requests, discussions, wiki pages, and communications related to the project.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainer through GitHub.
