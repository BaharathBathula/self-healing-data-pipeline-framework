# Slide 1 — Enterprise Reliability Challenge

## Problem

Modern enterprise systems increasingly depend on:

- distributed cloud-native infrastructure
- real-time analytics platforms
- AI-native operational systems
- streaming enterprise architectures

As operational complexity increases, traditional reactive monitoring systems become increasingly insufficient.

---

## Enterprise Challenges

- delayed anomaly detection
- manual incident recovery
- fragmented operational visibility
- SLA instability
- governance-aware operational risks
- operational scalability limitations

---

## Key Insight

Modern enterprise systems require:

✅ autonomous remediation  
✅ reliability intelligence  
✅ governance-aware orchestration  
✅ self-healing operational resilience
